## The board — what to build, cut, or charge for

### Put a number in the headline  (High impact, 1h)
**Problem:** Headlines make claims with no figure behind them.
**Do:** Lead with the measurable outcome, keep the audience in the subhead.

### Put spacing on a single step  (High impact, 1d)
**Problem:** Spacing does not follow any consistent unit, so vertical rhythm never settles.
**Do:** Adopt one base step and express every margin, padding and gap as a multiple of it.

### Fix the contrast failures  (Medium impact, 3h)
**Problem:** Text was found sitting below the contrast floor against its own background.
**Do:** Adjust the foreground or the surface until each pairing clears 4.5 to 1.

### Bring the small controls up to 24 pixels  (Medium impact, 2h)
**Problem:** Interactive elements were found under the minimum target size.
**Do:** Expand the hit area with padding, leaving the visual size alone.

### Leave one primary action above the fold  (High impact, 2h)
**Problem:** Several controls carry equal visual weight on the first screen.
**Do:** Promote one action and demote the rest to text links or outlined buttons.
